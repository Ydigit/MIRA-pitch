Amazon and UPS have spent years trying to make drone delivery pay.

The obstacle is the route. Nobody has a precise 3D picture of the city, so drones fly high over everything, and regulators keep the rules tight.

1

Digital twin

Drone scans become a live 3D model with Gaussian Splatting. Computer vision spots what changed, like a new crane.

2

Safe airspace

The twin becomes a 3D map of free space, with clearance margins, local rules and no-fly zones, in any country.

3

Deterministic routing

Like flight planning for aircraft: the same inputs always give the same deconflicted route, checked against the map.

4

AI agents on watch

They follow traffic, weather and events and propose re-routes, holds or recalls. Each decision passes the safety checks and is logged.
